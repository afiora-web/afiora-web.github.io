Privacy Policy — Diamond Art

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Diamond Art is a puzzle game published by Afiora. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. It is short because the app does very little.

We do not collect personal information

Diamond Art has no account, no sign-up and no login. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number, and we do not operate a server that receives your data. The only information about your play that reaches us is the anonymous, aggregated level statistics described under Analytics below.

Your photos stay on your device

The app can turn a photo from your gallery into a puzzle. When you choose a photo:

  • It is read and converted entirely on your device.
  • It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • It is not stored by the app and not shared.
  • It is discarded as soon as the puzzle is generated.

The app asks the Android system photo picker for one image at a time. It does not request permission to browse your photo library.

What is stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally, in the app's own private storage. It never leaves your phone and is deleted when you uninstall:

  • Which level you have reached
  • Whether you have seen the how-to-play guide
  • Your sound setting
  • Whether you have been asked to rate the app

Advertising

The app is free and is funded by advertising. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google may collect and process data including:

  • Your device's advertising identifier
  • Device information such as model, operating system version and language
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • How you interact with ads shown in the app

We do not receive this data and we cannot identify you from it. How Google uses it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent screen the first time you open it. You can accept or refuse personalised advertising there.
  • If you are in one of those regions, you can change your answer at any time in the app: Pause → Settings → Privacy choices.
  • On any device, you can reset or delete your advertising identifier in Settings → Google → Ads, and turn on Opt out of Ads Personalisation.

Children

Diamond Art is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will act on it — although, as described above, the app has no mechanism to receive personal information in the first place.

Analytics

To understand which levels players find too hard, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records:

  • When a level starts and when it is finished, with the level's name
  • Basic app usage, such as when the app is opened and how long a session lasts
  • Device information such as model, operating system version, app version and language
  • An approximate location (country or region), derived by Google from a masked IP address
  • A random identifier for this installation of the app, used to count players

We see these only as totals — for example, how many players finished level 5. It does not include your name, contacts, photos or anything you type, and the app does not give your advertising identifier to Analytics. Data is encrypted in transit.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Analytics stores no identifier on your device and sends only anonymous, cookieless signals. How Google handles this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in Google Analytics' data disclosure for apps.

Third-party services used

  • Google AdMob — advertising. Privacy policy
  • Google User Messaging Platform — the advertising consent screen
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — level statistics, see Analytics. Privacy policy
  • Google Play Services — required by the above

The app also uses your device's built-in text-to-speech to say short words of encouragement. This runs on the device and sends nothing to us.
